Privacy Policy

Lily Draft Uploader · Updated 26 July 2026

Lily Draft Uploader ("the tool") is a private, single-operator utility. It uploads finished video files to the TikTok drafts of one account — @_by_lily — which is owned and operated by the same person who runs the tool. It has no public users, no sign-up, and no user-facing service.

What the tool accesses

With the account owner's explicit authorization through TikTok's standard consent screen, the tool requests only two permissions:

  • user.info.basic — to confirm which account is connected (open ID, display name, username).
  • video.upload — to place video files into that account's drafts. The tool cannot publish; every post is reviewed and published manually from within the TikTok app.

What is stored, and where

TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored in a single file on the operator's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to a server, database, or cloud service belonging to the tool. There is no hosted backend.

The video files themselves are created by the operator and are sent directly to TikTok's upload endpoint.

What is not done

  • No data is collected from any person other than the account owner.
  • No data is sold, rented, shared, or transferred to third parties.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising, or profiling.
  • No scraping of other TikTok users, their content, or their followers.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use. Access can be withdrawn at any time from TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions, which immediately invalidates them. Deleting the local token file removes them from the computer.

AI-generated content

Lily is a fictional character and the content is AI-generated. Videos are labelled as AI-generated content when published, in line with TikTok's synthetic media policy. No real, identifiable person is depicted.

Changes

If the tool's behaviour changes, this page is updated and the date above is revised.
